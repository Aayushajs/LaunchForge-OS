Frequently Asked Questions

Is LaunchForge free?

Yes. It is open source under the Apache-2.0 license. You only pay for the server you run it on, and Oracle Cloud's free tier is enough to start.

What do I need to run it?

A Linux server with Ubuntu or Debian, 2 GB of RAM and ports 80 and 443 open. A domain is recommended for HTTPS.

How is it different from Heroku, Render or Railway?

It does the same job, but on servers you own: your data stays with you, and your bill is the price of the server, not of each app.

Can other people sign up on my server?

Yes. Turn on public sign-up and every new account gets its own isolated workspace with a resource quota, like a hosted platform.

How do updates work?

Every change to LaunchForge is released automatically with a version number. Update from the dashboard or with one command; the database is backed up first.
